# Evidence standard

Map every material criterion to a check before reaching a verdict. Record the criterion, the observable result it requires, the command or inspection used, and the evidence obtained.

Classify evidence by what it establishes:

- **Direct evidence:** the check exercises or inspects the criterion itself and observes the required result.
- **Indirect evidence:** the result supports the criterion through a related behavior or proxy but does not exercise it completely.
- **Absence of evidence:** the required check was not run, unavailable, unsafe without writes, inconclusive, or did not cover the criterion.
- **Contradictory evidence:** an observed result conflicts with the criterion or shows a relevant failure.

A passing command supports only the behaviors it actually exercises. Do not convert indirect or absent evidence into a pass. Preserve exact failures and distinguish related failures from evidence shown to be unrelated.
